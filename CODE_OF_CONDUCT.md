# Code of conduct

Assume the person on the other side is doing their best with less context than
you have. That covers nearly all of it.

Be direct about the code and easy on the person. A patch can be wrong, a rule
here can be wrong, and saying so plainly is the whole point of a review — but
the subject is always the change, never whoever wrote it. Harassment, personal
remarks, and anything you would not say to somebody's face are not welcome, and
neither is the sort of contempt that hides behind being technically correct.

Bug reports are a favour somebody is doing this project, usually on a bad day
after losing an hour to something. Read them that way.

If somebody behaves badly, tell the maintainer — through the Security tab's
private reporting, or in an issue if that is not open to you. Reports are
handled quietly. The maintainer will edit, close or delete what does not belong
here, and will block anyone who makes this an unpleasant place to be.
