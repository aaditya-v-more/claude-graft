New in 2.0Claude Code carries on when an account runs out

1× is less but 5× is too much.

Or 20× is less, lol.

One Claude login is never quite the right amount. Claude Graft runs every Claude account you have side by side, each signed in on its own, and lets them share their usage, so when one runs out, Claude Code carries on with another's.

Shared usage

When one account runs out, another answers.

Under Shared usage, add another of your Claudes or sign in to an account. Claude Code carries on with that account's allowance without leaving the Claude you are in. Only the model's answers come from the other account.

Picks the account with room

One under 98% of its five hours and 99% of its week, and the soonest weekly reset first.

Keeps a conversation where it is

A conversation stays with the account answering it while its cache lives, so nothing cached is spent twice.

Lets you choose

Pick the account for any conversation from the menu bar. Your choice holds until it has been quiet for a day.

Moves only the answers

Chats, files, artifacts and connectors stay with the Claude's own account, and the Chat tab is never shared.

Sharing needs macOS 15. Remove Graft and each Claude's Code settings go back as they were.

The menu bar

Always know who's answering.

Graft's item shows the Claude in front and its account's usage. While it shares, every account it draws on sits beside it, the one answering ringed in red, and the conversation on screen runs across the bar.

The Claude in front

Its icon and its account's usage, with its name too if you like. A plan with only a weekly limit shows its week, as W 94%.

Every account it draws on

In the order it draws on them, its own first. The one answering the conversation in front is full size in a red ring; the rest wait small and faint.

The conversation on screen

Its name runs across the bar with the account answering it, about a second after you switch to it. An older chat no account has answered yet shows by its name alone.

Your pick, when you want one

Choose which account answers any conversation from the dropdown. A tick marks the one in force.

Tidy by itself

Archive or delete a conversation and it leaves the bar and the dropdown.

Shortcuts

A Claude for every account.

New Shortcut makes a small app that opens Claude on a profile of its own. Sign in once and it stays signed in. Launch it from Spotlight, keep it in the Dock, and tell it apart by a colour no other Claude wears.

A new Claude starts with chats of its own. Reading another Claude's chats instead is a choice you make on its page, and then they are shared both ways.

Each one carries its own launcher, so it opens whether or not Graft is running.

Artifacts, projects and chats

Every account, in one place.

Sign in to each account once, with the code claude.ai emails you, and see all of their artifacts, projects and chats together. Each sign-in is kept apart for its account, and Graft never reads it.

Open any artifact, copy its link, make it private or public, rename, duplicate or delete it, and export it as Markdown, PDF, Word or page source. Share one with a group you keep in Graft, each person as a viewer, commenter or editor.

Every account's projects together, each a click away on claude.ai.

Search every account's chats and Claude Code sessions at once, and filter by where a session ran or whether a chat is archived. A search goes out when you press Return, never as you type.

Safe by design

Careful with what matters.

Graft borrows as little as it can and destroys nothing. Updating keeps every Claude, setting and chat exactly as it was.

Borrowed, never taken

Graft reads only each Claude's access token, writes nothing back, and sends it to api.anthropic.com alone.

Hands off what it didn't make

It knows its own apps by a file they carry, never by name, and Claude's own profile can't be deleted from it.

Sign-ins kept sealed

A claude.ai sign-in made in Graft lives in a store of its own for that account. Graft never reads it, and signing out removes it.

Updates that check themselves

Graft updates itself quietly, and every download is checked against a signing key that ships inside the app. Anything that doesn't match is refused.

Nothing measured

No account to make, no licence to buy, and nothing about you sent anywhere. It is free and MIT licensed.

Leaves cleanly

Remove the Homebrew cask and Graft takes its sharing back out of every Claude before it goes.

Install

Two minutes, no account.

Graft lives in the menu bar and keeps itself up to date. It needs macOS 13 and Claude Desktop; sharing usage needs macOS 15.

$ brew install --cask aaditya-v-more/claude-graft/claude-graft
or
Download the disk image · 2.0.1

Graft is ad-hoc signed rather than notarised, so the first open goes through System Settings › Privacy & Security; the cask does that for you. Every release is on GitHub.

Free, and staying that way.

No licence to buy, no account to make, nothing measured and sent anywhere. It exists because running two Claude accounts was annoying enough to fix properly.

If it saved you the trouble, there's a tip jar, and that's what keeps it worked on: new macOS releases break things, and Claude Desktop moves its own furniture about every few weeks.
