Update when you are ready

Graft has always installed its own updates as soon as they appear. That is still the default, but you can now turn on Hold Claude Graft updates in the menu bar or in Settings. Graft keeps checking every hour and shows a new version in the menu and on its icon, but downloads nothing until you press Install. Turn the hold off and updates go back to installing themselves.

Opening a shortcut you have renamed now opens that profile. Before, Graft could fail to find the renamed shortcut and open your main Claude instead.

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel, with macOS 13 or later supported.
