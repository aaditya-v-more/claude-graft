Accounts without a five-hour limit

Some plans now have only a weekly limit. Usage for those accounts had stopped updating, and where it did show, the five-hour figure read 0%. Graft now says an account has no five-hour limit in place of that bar, leaves out Start Session for it, and shows its weekly figure in the menu bar with a W in front, so W 94% means 94% of the week. Accounts that still have a five-hour limit are shown as before, and the menu bar no longer passes over an account without one when choosing whose figure to show.

Pinned chats sync between profiles again with current versions of Claude.

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel, with macOS 13 or later supported.
