Share usage between your accounts

A Claude can now let its Claude Code draw on your other accounts when its own allowance runs low, without signing out of anything. Add another of your Claudes under Shared usage, or sign in to an account from Graft without making a shortcut for it. Only the model's answers come from the other accounts; your chats, artifacts and connectors stay with the Claude's own account, and the Chat tab is never shared. The menu bar shows every account a Claude draws on and rings the one answering in red, and the conversation on screen runs across it with the account answering it, about a second after you switch to it. An older chat that no account has answered yet shows by its name alone, and archiving or deleting a conversation takes it off the bar. The dropdown lets you choose which account answers each conversation, with a tick beside the choice in force. Sharing needs macOS 15.

Artifacts, projects and search across accounts

Three new panes reach claude.ai for every account you sign in to there. Artifacts lists them all together: open one, copy its link, make it private or public, rename, duplicate or delete it, and export it as Markdown, PDF, a Word document or its page source. Share an artifact with a group you keep in Graft, and each person is invited as a viewer, commenter or editor. Projects lists every account's projects, and Search Chats searches every account's chats and Claude Code sessions at once, with filters for where a session ran and whether a chat is archived. Each account signs in once, with the code claude.ai emails you, in a window of Graft's; the sign-in is kept apart for that account and is never read by Graft.

A new window

The window has a proper sidebar, and every page fits at its smallest size without scrolling. Every Claude and every lent account now gets a colour of its own, so no two look alike; the Claudes you already have keep theirs.

A new Claude now starts with chats of its own, and sharing another Claude's chats is a choice you make on its page. Claudes you already have keep reading what they read before.

The Help menu now leads to the site, the release list, a new issue, and an email that already carries your Graft and macOS versions.

Accounts without a five-hour limit

Some plans now have only a weekly limit. Usage for those accounts had stopped updating, and where it did show, the five-hour figure read 0%. Graft now says an account has no five-hour limit in place of that bar, leaves out Start Session for it, and shows its weekly figure in the menu bar with a W in front, so W 94% means 94% of the week.

Pinned chats sync between profiles again with current versions of Claude, sessions from another Mac are left out of the sidebar, and Claude's updater now stays hidden and signed out while it installs.

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel, with macOS 13 or later supported.

Upgrading from 1.0.x

As with 1.1.0, shared profiles merge their chat histories on their next launch. Going back to a profile's own chats preserves what it brought, but copies already merged into the source stay there. If you need an untouched copy, back up your Claude profile folders in ~/Library/Application Support before upgrading.
