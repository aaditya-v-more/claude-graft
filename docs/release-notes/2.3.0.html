Accounts

Every account now has a page of its own in the sidebar: what each has used and when its windows reset, which Claudes draw on it, and when its login runs out, with Sign In Again beside one that has. An account signed in from Graft, with no Claude of its own here, shows its own usage too.

The same page sets the order accounts are used in under Shared usage. Left alone, the account whose week ends soonest goes first; move any account up, down or to the front, and By weekly reset puts the order back.

A shorter dropdown, and a list by Claude

The menu bar dropdown draws an account's five-hour, weekly and Fable usage side by side, in larger type, so each account takes three lines where it took five or more, and its scroll bar no longer takes a strip of the width. By Claude, on the switch above the list, lists each Claude with every account it draws on under Shared usage, each by its address; an account lent to several Claudes is listed under each, and a session is started from the Claude's own line. By account is the list of every account once, as before.

An account signed in from Graft whose login cannot be asked for its usage now gets its figures, each model's week included, through the same account's sign-in under Artifacts, when the dropdown or Accounts opens or you refresh it.

Auto-start

An account with Auto-start on has its next five-hour window opened as soon as the last one closes, so the window is already running down when you sit down to work. It is off until you switch it on, one account at a time, in the dropdown or under Accounts, and it says beside the switch when the next session starts or why one could not. An account has the one switch wherever it is listed.

A conversation stays on its account

Under Shared usage a conversation used to drift to another account between turns once it had sat for a few minutes, losing its cache and its reasoning each time. It now starts on the account of the Claude you opened it in, stays there until that account runs out, and then stays wherever it went. A conversation you move from the dropdown stays where you put it. A side chat, asked with /btw, is answered by the account answering the conversation you asked it in. To have new conversations start on the first account in your order instead, turn off “Keep a conversation in the Claude it started in” in Settings.

Room is counted for the model being asked for. An account out of one model, or on a plan that sells that model apart, is passed over for that model alone and still takes every other.

More than one organization

An address that belongs to several organizations can choose which one Artifacts, Projects and Search Chats show, and each organization counts as an account of its own under Shared usage.

Also fixed

  • Graft left running for many hours no longer grows by gigabytes, and the conversation in the menu bar no longer swaps between two copies of the same chat every two seconds.
  • The figure in the menu bar is the one for the account answering the conversation in front.
  • On macOS 27 a stray mark could be left showing beside the conversation in the menu bar. It no longer can.
  • A program that a Claude Code session starts no longer fails its certificate check while Shared usage is on.
  • With nothing to sync, Graft no longer rewrites its record of shared chats on every pass.

With a fix from sohaibahsan007.

On Windows

This release is for the Mac. Windows stays on 2.2.0.

Upgrading from 1.0.x

As with 1.1.0, shared profiles merge their chat histories on their next launch. Going back to a profile's own chats preserves what it brought, but copies already merged into the source stay there. If you need an untouched copy, back up your Claude profile folders in ~/Library/Application Support before upgrading.
